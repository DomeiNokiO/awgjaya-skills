# Banned vocabulary, phrases, openers (English)

Statistically flagged as AI markers (Carnegie Mellon 2025, Wikipedia "Signs of AI Writing", Buffer 52M-post analysis). Never use; replace with a concrete alternative or restructure.

## Banned vocabulary
delve/delves/delving, tapestry, landscape (figurative), testament ("a testament to"), vibrant, pivotal, crucial, intricate/intricacies, meticulous/meticulously, bolster/bolstered, garner/garnered, underscore/underscores, interplay, multifaceted, nuanced (filler), foster/fostering, leverage (verb), utilize (say "use"), commence (say "start"), facilitate, encompass/encompassing, paramount, groundbreaking, cutting-edge, game-changing/game-changer, transformative, revolutionize, seamless/seamlessly, robust (outside engineering), comprehensive (of own output), endeavor, aforementioned, harnessing, spearheading, navigating (figurative), showcasing, highlighting, emphasizing, enhancing, unprecedented, remarkable, stunning, profound, epic (non-literal), in essence, thought leader/leadership, synergy/synergies, pain points, value add/value proposition (casual), moving forward, touch base/circle back, rest assured, it goes without saying.

**Current-era survivors (weight these heaviest):** emphasizing, enhance, highlighting, showcasing.

## Banned phrases
"In today's [adj] [noun]…", "It's worth noting that…", "It's important to note that…", "Let's dive in/deeper/delve into", "At its core…", "In the realm of…", "When it comes to…", "A testament to…", "Not just X, but Y", "It's not just about X — it's about Y", "This is where X comes in", "Whether you're a X or a Y…", "From X to Y" (range opener), "At the end of the day…", "The bottom line is…", "Here's the thing/deal…", "Without further ado…", "In a nutshell…", "Buckle up", "Take it to the next level", "Unlock the power of…", "Empower/empowering", "Elevate your…", "Streamline your…", "Supercharge your…", "Bridge the gap", "Move the needle", "In conclusion", "Overall," (starter), "Firstly… Secondly… Thirdly…", "I hope this helps", "I hope this email finds you well", "As per my last email", "Please don't hesitate to reach out".

## Banned openers
"Certainly,", "Absolutely,", "Sure,", "Great question!", "That's a great point!", "I'd be happy to…", "As an AI…", "As a language model…", "However, it's important to…", "Moreover,", "Furthermore,", "Additionally,", "Interestingly,", "Notably,", "Importantly,", "Indeed,".

## Model first-word tells (avoid opening with)
ChatGPT: as, yes, sure, here, in, to, creating, certainly, title, the. Claude: in, from, this, how, yes, title, according, the, based, here. Grok: step, introduction, yes, creating, to, title, in, certainly. Gemini: my, creating, while, here, yes, this, the. DeepSeek: based, yes, step, comprehensive, here, to, creating, title, certainly.

## Structural tells (prose)
- Rule of three; uniform sentence length (3+ same-length in a row); parataxis (chained short declaratives).
- Hedging seesaw; contrast framing ("not X but Y"); binary framing ("the question isn't X, it's Y").
- False agency ("the data shows/tells"); present-participle padding; inflating adverbs that survive removal (importantly, fundamentally, remarkably, arguably, notably).
- Synonym cycling (rotating "the framework/this library/Meta's creation" — keep the clearest name).
- Vague attribution ("experts agree", "studies show" with no named source).
- Rhetorical abuse: self-answered questions, mid-sentence pivot ("The solution? Simpler than you think."), mic-drop closers, performed authenticity ("I'll be honest,").
- 2026 model-tell panel: colon-elaboration density, semicolon uptick, affirmative pivot ("it's not just X, it's Y"), staccato triplet ("No X. No Y. Just Z."), fixed collocations ("plays a crucial role"), title-case-everywhere + emoji section markers + a horizontal rule between every section.
