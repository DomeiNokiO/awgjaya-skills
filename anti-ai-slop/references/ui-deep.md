# UI deep reference — Hallmark named tells, color/type systems, scoring gates

Sources: Nutlope/hallmark (full read), dtransla-maker/Anti-AI-Design-Slop (10-axis scoring), prodigeproject/prodigeui (positive gate). Load this when doing serious UI build/audit/redesign; `ui-rules.md` is the fast catalog, this is the depth.

## The positive gate (the correction most skills miss)
Avoiding slop is **necessary but NOT sufficient**. A technique is not slop or craft by its name — glassmorphism, parallax, big gradients, decorative motion, huge type, full-viewport heroes are **slop when purposeless/generic, craft when intentional and well-executed**. Do not blanket-ban techniques (that would reject best-in-class reference sites). Flag the **absence of intent and execution**, not the presence of a technique. Two things must BOTH hold: (1) avoids the generic AI defaults (negative gate), (2) actually demonstrates craft — advanced technique, choreography, texture, distinctive typography (positive gate). Systematic fluid values (`clamp(2.5rem,14vw,17.5rem)`, `translateX(-50%)`, viewport units, role-based scroll offsets) are craft, not raw-value violations; only *arbitrary* one-offs (`padding:13px`, `#4a4a4a` at random) fail. Judge "part of a coherent system?" not "is this a token literal?".

## Color system (OKLCH) — hallmark
- **OKLCH only** (perceptually uniform; hsl/rgb lie about brightness). Four layers: **Paper** (base surface, L96–98% light / 12–16% dark), **Ink** (text, L16–22% light / 92–96% dark), **Neutrals** (5–9 steps, anchor chroma tint 0.005–0.015), **Accent** (one saturated, chroma 0.12–0.22).
- **One accent, max two.** Accent occupies **≤3% of any viewport** — a highlighter, never a background block. Uses: active nav item, focus ring, link-hover underline, primary CTA border/text, a small square beside a heading. Never fill giant buttons or whole sections.
- **Tint the greys** toward the anchor hue (warm accent → warm neutrals). Flat zero-chroma grey is a tell.
- **Dark mode:** Paper L12–18% (not #000), Ink L92–96% (not #fff); reduce body weight ~50 units; accent −chroma 0.02–0.04, +lightness 5–10%; higher surfaces are *lighter* not darker (+~3% L/level); never switch hue between modes — only L and C move.
- **Bans:** pure #000/#fff, flat grey, purple→cyan / purple→blue / orange→pink gradients, three-stop gradients (two-stop only), accent as >5% fill, grey text on colored bg, red–green as the only signal (add icon/pattern).
- Contrast: body 4.5:1 (target 7:1), large text 3:1 (target 4.5:1), UI boundaries 3:1, placeholder 4.5:1. Prefer APCA; verify with vision-deficiency emulator.

## Typography — hallmark
- **2+1 rule:** at most three families — one display, one body, optional outlier for ONE moment (wordmark / hero stat / pull quote), appearing in ≤2 places. Two is canonical, four is slop. Mono counts as a family; same family at different weights is one.
- **Commit to extremes:** weight 200 next to 800 reads intentional; 400 next to 600 reads like a default. Headings contrast body by ≥300 weight units. Never synthesize bold/italic — load the real weight.
- **Size = ratio not increment** (1.25 major third default). Max 5 sizes/page; add hierarchy with weight+color, not more sizes. Display cap ≤5.5rem (88px), 6rem on manifesto/brutal; default `clamp(2.75rem,5vw+1rem,5.25rem)`. Match hero size to copy length — a 100-char headline at display size is a top AI tell; write your own headlines ≤7 words / ≤50 chars, imperative or nominal, never a gerund opener.
- **Line-height:** display 1.05–1.2, body 1.5–1.65; all-caps display floor 1.0 (rec 1.02–1.08). Measure 45–75ch (`max-width:65ch`). Never all-caps body, never justified without hyphenation, never letter-spacing >0.05em on body.
- **Banned default fonts** (on-distribution for every LLM): Inter, Roboto, Open Sans, Lato, Poppins, Source Sans, Nunito, Montserrat, Raleway, Work Sans, DM Sans, system-only, Arial, Helvetica; Merriweather, Playfair-as-body, Lora, Source Serif; Courier New, Consolas-default. Use only with a deliberate reason.
- **Good free faces (Google/Fontshare):** display — Fraunces, Newsreader, Instrument Serif, Cormorant/EB Garamond, DM Serif Display, Bodoni Moda, Geist, Bricolage Grotesque, Space Grotesk, Anton, Cabinet/Clash Display, Tanker; body — Geist, Newsreader, Source Serif 4, Spectral, Crimson Pro, IBM Plex Sans, Switzer, General Sans; mono — Geist Mono, JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Mono, Commit Mono, Space Mono. Never name a paid/foundry font in code unless the user confirmed a licence (falls back to system-default = looks broken).
- **Required:** `font-display:swap`, fallback-metric overrides (`size-adjust`/`ascent-override`…) to kill CLS, `tabular-nums` on data columns, curly quotes `“ ” ‘ ’`, real `—` and `…` (never `--` / `...`). Wordmark may (and on editorial should) use a different display face than body. **Headers are roman** — an italic emphasis-word in a heading (`Built to <em>think</em>`) is one of the most reliable AI tells.

## Structural discipline (the differentiator)
Two pages for two briefs must feel like **different sites, not color-swaps of one template**. Before coding, look for a prior `/* macrostructure: <name> */` stamp and pick a **categorically different** one. Do not default to "Specimen" (numbered left-margin labels + huge serif + asymmetric spans) unless the brief is explicitly editorial/foundry — it's the single most-repeated AI-editorial output. For vague briefs pick from: Bento Grid, Long Document, Marquee Hero, Stat-Led, Workbench, Conversational FAQ, Manifesto, Photographic, Quote-Led. **Eyebrows default OFF** — valid only when content is genuinely numbered/chaptered, cap 1–2/page; the tag-left/header-right two-column section head is a hard-banned editorial-SaaS tell (heading goes directly under the eyebrow, same column). Parity instructions do NOT bypass this — drop inherited eyebrows silently.

## Motion tells (fix each)
`transition-all` → name the properties. Universal `hover:scale-105` → one signal per element. Bouncy/overshoot easing on UI → exponential ease-out (reserve overshoot for drag-release). Animated hover gradients, cursor-follower dots → cut. Auto-rotating carousel with no pause → WCAG 2.2.2 fail; manual/pause-on-hover-and-focus. Celebratory success toasts → silent success (toast only failures/async/explicit confirms). Confirm dialog for reversible action → optimistic + Undo toast; modal only for irreversible, and then type-the-name. Tooltip same delay hover+focus → hover 800–1000ms, focus 0ms. Focus rings that animate in → instant, always. Toasts that shift layout → fixed-corner stack. Universal scroll fade-up → one orchestrated entrance, rest just there. Spinner flash → delay-show 150ms or min-visible 300ms; prefer skeletons. Keep UI motion <300ms.

## More named tells (hallmark critical/major/minor)
**Critical:** purple-gradient hero, gradient headline (`background-clip:text`), Inter-everywhere, 3-column feature grid, card-in-card, side-stripe card (thick colored left edge), full-viewport centered hero, pure black/white, the AI nav (wordmark-left + 4–5 centered links + CTA-right + sticky hairline), the AI footer (Product/Company/Resources/Legal 4-col + social row + tiny copyright), aurora-blob background, floating-orb decoration, sound-on autoplay (always `muted loop playsinline`), lazy-loaded LCP (use `fetchpriority="high"`). **Major:** bounce/elastic easing, centered-everything, italic headers, eyebrow-on-every-section, shadow-glow on dark (elevate by lightness), icon-tile feature card, purposeless glassmorphism, hover-only affordances (need focus + tap), tabular data without `tabular-nums`, animate-on-scroll on everything, mismatched icon sets (one library/project), AI-illustration look (mesh-blob characters, corporate doodles), invented metrics (`10× faster`, `50,000+ teams` → use `—` + "metric to confirm" or ask), generic emoji as feature icon (✨🚀⚡), re-drawn UI chrome (fake browser/phone/IDE bars — use real screenshots in `<figure>`), Lottie shortcut (build CSS/SVG), Three.js for a still object. **Minor:** straight quotes, `--` dashes, `...`, placeholder names (Jane Doe), startup-cliche names (Acme/Nexus/Pulse), `z-index:9999`, uniform section padding, `100vw` widths.

## 10-axis review score (dtransla) — score each 0–5 before shipping
1 Originality ("logo removed — would anyone know whose this is?") · 2 Message clarity (point understood in 5s) · 3 Hierarchy (clear first/second/third; not too many sizes/colors) · 4 Motion & effects (removing it makes design weaker not stronger) · 5 Hover/interaction (makes next action more obvious; nothing essential hover-only) · 6 Readability/contrast (readable on a phone without zoom) · 7 Brand specificity (expresses category/personality; no generic AI icons/brain-network/3D-cubes/stock-robots) · 8 Buyer trust (serious buyer more comfortable) · 9 Mobile/feed performance (stops the right person; strong first frame) · 10 Intentionality (every element has a job). Any axis <3 → revise. Hallmark's own version: score P/H/E/S/R/V (Philosophy, Hierarchy, Execution, Specificity, Restraint, Variety) 1–5, anything <3 triggers a revision pass.

## Hard blocks (prodige FAIL — correctness/a11y, aesthetic-agnostic)
Raw values instead of tokens · normal text contrast <4.5:1 · large/UI contrast <3:1 · no visible focus ring (≥3:1) · off-scale spacing (not on 4px base) · no visual hierarchy (H1/H2/H3 same size+weight) · hardcoded colors instead of semantic roles · fewer than three product-specific visible decisions · declared webfont silently falling back after `document.fonts.ready` · entrance effects leaving content invisible under no-JS/reduced-motion · imitating benchmark geometry to satisfy the gate.
